What this prototype does not claim

• Detection results are precomputed samples, not a live model.
• Species candidates are probabilities, not a count of everything present.
• Figures such as the premium and costs are illustrative.
• The label needs a published method and independent verification before real use.

Built on, not invented

Open sound classifiers (BirdNET, Merlin Sound ID) exist. The novelty here is linking observations to verified labels, grower premiums, and school and sponsor participation.
